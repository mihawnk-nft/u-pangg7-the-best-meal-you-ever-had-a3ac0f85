An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-the-best-meal-you-ever-had-a3ac0f85)
bounty "The Best Meal You Ever Had" — write one sentence, at most 200
characters, about the best meal you ever had.

## The entry

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, no network, no input) writes one
sentence to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The best meal I ever had was my grandmother's rendang in Sidoarjo, dark and slow-cooked until it fell apart over warm rice, steam smelling of cloves while rain drummed on the tin roof above us.

193 characters, one sentence — a concrete memory, not general praise: a
named dish (rendang), a place (grandmother's kitchen in Sidoarjo), a
texture (dark, slow-cooked, falling apart over warm rice), a smell
(cloves in the steam), and one specific moment (rain drumming on the
tin roof above us).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
